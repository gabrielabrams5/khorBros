Khor Bros Menu Maker
====================

How to use:

  1. Unzip this folder somewhere easy to find (Desktop is fine).
  2. Double-click "Launch Menu Maker".
  3. A black window appears with a list of twist flavors.
       - Arrow keys: move up/down
       - Spacebar:   check/uncheck a flavor
       - Enter:      confirm your selection
       - Q:          cancel and quit
  4. When it finishes, your new menu image will be in the "generated"
     folder right next to the launcher. Filename starts with "DE.RM."

That's it.


First-time Mac warning
----------------------
The first time you double-click "Launch Menu Maker.command", macOS may
say "cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer."

To fix it once:
  1. Right-click (or Control-click) the file.
  2. Choose "Open" from the menu.
  3. Click "Open" in the dialog that appears.

You only have to do this the first time. Same applies to "KhorBrosMenu".


First-time Windows warning
--------------------------
Windows Defender SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC."

To run it:
  1. Click "More info".
  2. Click "Run anyway".

You only have to do this the first time.


Where do the menu images go?
----------------------------
A folder called "generated" appears right next to the launcher.
Every menu you make lands there. You can drag those JPGs straight
into email, Slack, or wherever you need them.
